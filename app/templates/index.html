Assumptions & Methodology +

Settlement SLA. Cleared funds arrive T+1 to T+2 calendar days after capture; October 30–31 timing gaps are valid next-month rollovers.

Rounding. The platform sums six-decimal 1.75% fee calculations before rounding; the bank rounds each row using ROUND_HALF_UP.

Matching. Captures match on transaction_id; refunds point to platform captures through parent_transaction_id. All timestamps are UTC and amounts are INR.
